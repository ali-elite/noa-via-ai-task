# Account Security

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
We highly recommend enabling 2FA for all accounts. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.) and SMS-based 2FA.

## Suspicious Activity
If you notice unrecognized logins or changes to your account data, reset your password immediately and contact Support. Our security team will review session logs.

## Data Deletion Requests
Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to request full deletion of your account data. You can submit a deletion request via our privacy portal. Deletion requests take up to 30 days to process and are irreversible.
