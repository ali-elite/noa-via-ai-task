# Subscription Management

## Billing
Subscriptions are billed automatically on a monthly or annual basis, depending on your selected plan. Billing occurs on the anniversary date of your initial sign-up.

## Upgrading or Downgrading
You can upgrade your subscription at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated.
Downgrades will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. We do not provide prorated refunds for downgrades during an active cycle.

## Cancellation
You may cancel your subscription at any time. Your access will remain active until the end of your current paid billing period. After cancellation, you will not be charged again. We do not offer partial refunds for the remainder of a billing cycle.
