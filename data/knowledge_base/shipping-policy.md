# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## General Shipping Times
Our standard shipping processing time is 1-2 business days.
Standard Delivery: 5-7 business days.
Expedited Delivery: 2-3 business days.
Overnight Delivery: Next business day (for orders placed before 2 PM EST).

## International Shipping
We currently ship to the US, Canada, UK, and the EU. International orders may be subject to customs and import duties, which are the responsibility of the customer.

## Lost or Undelivered Packages
If your tracking shows as "Delivered" but you have not received it:
1. Wait 24 hours (sometimes couriers scan early).
2. Check with neighbors or your building's reception.
3. Contact support with your Order Number and tracking ID. If the package cannot be located after 7 business days, a replacement will be issued. No refunds are granted for lost packages, only replacements.
