# Returns and Refunds Policy

## 30-Day Return Window
We accept returns for 30 days from the original date of delivery. Items must be in their original packaging, unused, and with tags attached.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Digital products (software, eBooks)
- Perishable goods
- Gift cards
- Clearance items marked as "Final Sale"

## Processing Refunds
Once your return is received and inspected (typically 3-5 business days after receipt), we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment.
Credit card refunds may take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement.

## Damaged Items
If you received a damaged or defective item, please contact support within 48 hours of delivery. We require photo evidence of the damage. For confirmed defects, we offer a full refund or free replacement, plus we cover return shipping costs.
