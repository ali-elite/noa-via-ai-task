# SLA and Response Times

## Overview
Our Service Level Agreement (SLA) outlines the expected response times for support inquiries based on their urgency level and your subscription tier.

## Urgency Levels

### Low Priority
General inquiries, feature requests, and non-critical product questions.
- **Basic Tier**: 48 hours
- **Pro Tier**: 24 hours
- **Enterprise Tier**: 12 hours

### Normal Priority
Issues that affect minor functionality but do not stop you from using our core services. Workarounds are typically available.
- **Basic Tier**: 24 hours
- **Pro Tier**: 12 hours
- **Enterprise Tier**: 4 hours

### High Priority
Critical functionality is impaired, causing significant impact to your workflow. No acceptable workaround exists.
- **Basic Tier**: 12 hours
- **Pro Tier**: 4 hours
- **Enterprise Tier**: 1 hour

### Urgent Priority
Complete service failure or critical security issues.
- **Basic Tier**: 4 hours
- **Pro Tier**: 1 hour
- **Enterprise Tier**: 15 minutes (24/7 coverage)

## Contact Methods
- **Basic and Pro Tiers**: Support tickets via the web portal or email.
- **Enterprise Tier**: Support tickets, email, phone support, and dedicated Slack channel.
