# Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance

## Our Commitment to Privacy
We are committed to protecting your personal data and complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws.

## Data Collection
We only collect data that is necessary to provide our services. This includes:
- Account information (Name, Email)
- Billing details (processed securely via Stripe)
- Usage metrics (to improve our product performance)

## Your Rights Under GDPR
If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:
- **Right to Access**: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification**: You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: You can request that delete your personal data.
- **Right to Data Portability**: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used format.

## Data Deletion Request Process
To exercise your right to erasure:
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll to the bottom and click "Delete Account and Data".
4. Confirm the prompt.

Please note that we may retain certain data for legal compliance or essential business operations (e.g., invoice records) as permitted by law.

## Sub-processors
We use trusted third-party sub-processors (like AWS for hosting and OpenAI for AI features). A full list is available in our DPA (Data Processing Agreement).
