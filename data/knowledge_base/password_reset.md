# Password Reset and Account Recovery Guide

## Forgot Password?
If you have forgotten your password, follow these steps to reset it:
1. Navigate to the Login page.
2. Click on the "Forgot Password?" link.
3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
4. You will receive an email with a secure link to reset your password.
5. Click the link and enter your new password.

**Note**: The password reset link expires after 2 hours for security reasons.

## Locked Accounts
Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. To unlock your account:
- **Wait**: The lock will automatically expire after 30 minutes.
- **Reset**: You can use the "Forgot Password?" flow to immediately unlock your account and reset the password.

## Lost Access to Email
If you no longer have access to the email address associated with your account, please contact our Support team. You will need to verify your identity by providing recent billing information or answering security questions before we can update the email address on file.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Recovery
If you lose your 2FA device, you can use one of the backup codes provided during setup. If you do not have backup codes, please contact our security team at security@example.com for manual verification.
