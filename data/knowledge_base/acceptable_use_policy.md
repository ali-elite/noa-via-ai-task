# Acceptable Use Policy

## Overview
This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) governs the use of our platform, APIs, and associated services. Violation of this policy may result in immediate suspension or termination of your account.

## Prohibited Activities
You may not use our services to:
1. **Engage in Illegal Activities**: Violate any local, national, or international laws or regulations.
2. **Distribute Malware**: Host, distribute, or execute malicious software, viruses, or ransomware.
3. **Spam or Phish**: Send unsolicited communications, spam, or engage in phishing campaigns.
4. **Harassment**: Harass, threaten, or abuse others, or promote discrimination and violence.
5. **Circumvent Security**: Attempt to bypass our security measures, rate limits, or access data you are not authorized to view.
6. **Crypto Mining**: Utilize our infrastructure for cryptocurrency mining without explicit written permission.

## Security Vulnerability Reporting
If you discover a security vulnerability in our platform, please report it to security@example.com. Do not exploit the vulnerability. We run a bug bounty program and will reward responsible disclosure.

## Enforcement
We monitor network traffic and API usage for suspicious activity. If we detect a violation, we reserve the right to:
- Issue a warning.
- Temporarily suspend access.
- Permanently terminate the account without a refund.
- Report activities to relevant law enforcement agencies.
