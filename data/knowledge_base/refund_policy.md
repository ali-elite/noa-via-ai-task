# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Overview
We strive to ensure complete satisfaction with our services. If you are not entirely satisfied with your subscription, we're here to help.

## Cancellation
You may cancel your subscription at any time through our billing portal. The cancellation will take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You will continue to have access to our services until that date.

## Refund Eligibility
- **Annual Subscriptions**: We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for annual subscriptions. If you request a refund within 14 days of your initial purchase, you will receive a full refund.
- **Monthly Subscriptions**: Monthly subscriptions are generally non-refundable unless there was a billing error or a significant service outage lasting more than 48 hours.
- **Enterprise Plans**: Refunds for enterprise plans are subject to the terms of your specific contract.

## Process
To request a refund, please open a support ticket with the category "Billing and Subscriptions". Please include your invoice number and the reason for the request.

## Exceptions
We reserve the right to deny refund requests if we detect abuse of our policy or violation of our Acceptable Use Policy.
