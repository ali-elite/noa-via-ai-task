# Technical Troubleshooting FAQ

## Login Issues
- **Forgot Password**: Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. An email with reset instructions will be sent.
- **Account Locked**: Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts. Locks automatically expire after 30 minutes. Alternatively, resetting your password instantly unlocks your account.
- **Not Receiving Emails**: Check the spam/junk folder. Ensure `noreply@company.com` is whitelisted.

## System Requirements
Our web platform requires a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) updated within the last 12 months.
For mobile applications, iOS 14.0+ and Android 10.0+ are supported.

## Slow Performance
To resolve performance issues:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Disable any adblocker or privacy extensions (some scripts might get blocked)
3. Try an Incognito window
If the issue persists, please submit a ticket with a PDF export of your browser console logs.
